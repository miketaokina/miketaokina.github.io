- Q: How to preview in local?  
  A: 1. cd ~/your local workspace<br>
       2. in terminal: python3 -m http.server 8000<br>
       3. Go to http://localhost:8000 in your browser<br>
       4. Control + C to terminate

     5. http://127.0.0.1:5500 will be opened in your default browser
